# Credit Risk and Loan Loss Analysis
### 1. Business scenario
This is a credit history of the customers from a financial institution. Agenda is to predict for possible credit defaulters upfront and help the financial institutions to take steps accordingly.  
The project manager requires the risk analytics team to evaluate the credit risk of historical loan portfolios based on borrowers’ credit histories and loan characteristics. The analysts are expected to identify factors associated with poor loan performance, assess the potential probability of default, and estimate credit losses. The analysis will provide data-driven insights to support credit risk assessment, lending decisions, capital planning, and compliance with regulatory requirements.
### 2. Business problem
The business problem is to develop suitable models and identify key factors that can help the bank assess loan performance and credit risk. These insights can support more informed credit risk assessment and lending decisions.
### 3. Key questions
- How many variables could be used for building a model and calculating metrics?
- What borrower and loan characteristics are associated with poor loan performance?
- How do credit risk and loan characteristics differ across loan grades and origination vintages?
